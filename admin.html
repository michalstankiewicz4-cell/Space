Read-only view of activity_log — a passive record of network-behavior anomalies (rate-limit trips, insert/delete bursts, nick-change spam, admin-secret brute-force attempts), not a ban list. Nothing here takes any action on its own; it's meant for a human to skim. Gated by a secret (never stored in this file or the repo) checked server-side — see supabase/schema.sql's admin_activity_log(). Nick/IP/browser are self-reported or ordinary HTTP headers, not verified identity — a distinguishing hint for the common case, never proof of who did something.
